# Privacy Policy for FloraFinder AI

**Last Updated:** August 30, 2026  
**Developer:** Dr. A. Alex Mohit  
**Application:** FloraFinder AI (iOS)

---

## 1. Overview
FloraFinder AI ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using FloraFinder AI on iOS and iPadOS devices. 

FloraFinder AI is engineered with a **local-first, privacy-by-design architecture**. We do not track users, sell personal data, or serve third-party advertisements.

---

## 2. Information We Access & Process

### A. Camera & Visual Data (Real-Time Vision)
* **Usage:** FloraFinder AI requests access to your camera (`NSCameraUsageDescription`) to perform live, real-time plant and leaf identification.
* **On-Device Processing:** Live camera frame buffers are analyzed locally on your device using Apple's Vision framework and the Apple Neural Engine. Live video feeds and camera streams are **never recorded, stored, or transmitted** to external servers during routine scanning.

### B. Photo Library
* **Usage:** When using "Plant Doctor Mode," you may optionally select photos from your device library (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`) to diagnose plant diseases and view care schedules.
* **Storage:** Selected photos remain stored locally on your device.

### C. Location Data (GPS)
* **Usage:** If granted permission (`NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription`), the App accesses your approximate or precise location solely to geotag botanical discoveries in your offline **Field Journal**.
* **Storage:** All location data is stored exclusively on your device's local storage sandbox. We do not collect, aggregate, or transmit your location history.

### D. AI Cloud Diagnostics (Google Gemini API)
* **Usage:** In "Plant Doctor Mode," when you request an advanced botanical pathology triage, the selected plant image and symptom query are transmitted securely via HTTPS to Google's Gemini 2.0 API solely to generate diagnostic reports and care steps.
* **Data Handling:** Queries are not associated with personal identifiers, account credentials, or tracking identifiers.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
* **No Tracking:** We do not track you across apps or websites.
* **No Data Brokerage:** We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.
* **No Advertising SDKs:** The App contains zero advertising networks, third-party analytics trackers, or user profiling frameworks.

---

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion
All journal entries, saved specimens, and custom notes are stored locally on your iOS device. You may delete any saved specimen or clear your journal at any time directly within the App, or by uninstalling the application.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
FloraFinder AI is suitable for all ages (Rated 4+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
